Top 5 Red Flags of Online Job Scams

  • 1. Upfront Fees: Asking for registration fees, training materials, or ID card charges.
  • 2. Check / Wire Scams: Sending you a check to purchase equipment from their "vendor".
  • 3. Chat Only Hiring: Hiring you exclusively through Telegram or WhatsApp without video calls.
  • 4. Unrealistic Pay: Offering $50+/hr for simple data entry, typing, or rating videos.
  • 5. Free Email Domains: Claiming to represent Google or Amazon from a @gmail.com or @yahoo.com address.
